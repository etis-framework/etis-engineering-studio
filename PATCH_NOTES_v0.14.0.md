# ETIS Engineering Studio v0.14.0 Overlay

## Purpose

v0.14 turns the student evidence surface into a usable engineering workspace and makes review sessions behave more like recurring office-hours/review-board conversations with senior engineers.

## Student experience

- `Evidence Map` is renamed **Engineering Evidence**.
- Students see evidence-supported strengths before deficiencies.
- Professional lenses now expose related team evidence and findings instead of only displaying definitions.
- Evidence items explain current-phase scope, baseline/team provenance, equivalent evidence, and status.
- Any evidence item can seed a Focused Review.
- Any current finding can seed a Finding Review.
- Related GitHub evidence opens in a new browser tab.
- `Commit My Position` becomes contextual **State My Recommendation**.
- Finding Reviews do not force a recommendation.
- Completing one review preserves the evidence baseline and makes it natural to begin a different review purpose.

## Engine behavior

- Focused Review is now a genuine consultative review of a student-selected artifact/decision/concern.
- Senior reviewers are explicitly instructed to give an honest evidence-grounded opinion when students ask “what do you think?” or “is this good enough?” rather than hiding behind endless questions.
- Cross-session coaching continuity is available, but prior sessions never count as proof for a new engineering claim.
- Identical commit + phase reviews share the same frozen evidence snapshot and finding lifecycle memory.
- Evidence packages expose phase scope, equivalent-path, and URL metadata to the semantic reviewer.

## War-game expansion

The student corpus now includes 111 scenarios and the teaching-staff corpus 29, adding international/multilingual phrasing, culturally direct/indirect communication, work-in-progress artifact reviews, cross-session transitions, evidence/finding confusion, profanity/frustration, product confusion, and requests for candid senior advice.
