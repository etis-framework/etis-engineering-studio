# Course Source Manifest

> **Status:** Current Wave 1 course-source precedence for COMP 330/474 Fall 2026.

The Studio course model was derived from the authoritative Fall 2026 COMP 330 materials supplied by the instructor, including:

- undergraduate syllabus and schedule;
- A1-A6 Sakai assignment instructions;
- detailed assignment packages;
- phase-gate engineering review rubrics;
- release/operational-readiness rubric;
- Team Project Overview and CampusConnect brief;
- team operating model and cycle expectations;
- repository setup/workflow/README guidance;
- team peer-evaluation form;
- Definition of Done and supporting engineering guidance.

## Source precedence encoded in the Studio

1. **Sakai** — required course work, dates, grading, submission expectations.
2. **GitHub team repository** — authoritative team engineering evidence and workflow history once the exact repository is verified.
3. **ETIS Engineering Platform/reference guidance** — broader professional reference used to deepen judgment without silently expanding assignment requirements.

The Studio must not treat starter-kit scaffold or canonical filenames as proof that required engineering work has been completed.

## Source cleanup rule

Known assignment-package copy-forward or command-order defects are not intentionally reproduced as product semantics. When course-source ambiguities matter to student requirements, Sakai/instructor direction remains authoritative rather than AI inference.
