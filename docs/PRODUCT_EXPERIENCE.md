# Product Experience

## Student: Engineering Review Room

The main surface is intentionally active. The student sees:

- Review Context Bar: team, project, phase, repository state.
- Current Engineering Question: the professional question for the lifecycle gate.
- Review Board: specialist lenses and Chief Architect challenge.
- Evidence Rail: exactly what evidence is visible to the current review.
- Decision Posture: approve, conditional approval, defer, reject, constrain, request evidence, escalate.
- Defense Composer: decision + tradeoff + evidence + uncertainty + consequences + ownership + change trigger.
- Learning Signals: whether the student made professional engineering moves; not a grade.

## Scenario mode

Scenarios are not trivia questions. They inject a credible change in business or technical context and force a decision under constraints. A scenario can expose a student who has memorized terminology but cannot apply judgment.

Examples in Wave 1 include scope pressure, false precision in estimates, hidden dependencies, AI-generated planning, single-point ownership, and artifact theater.

## What happens when a student does not defend a decision

The Studio does not immediately mark the student “wrong.” It escalates the review:

1. Ask for the missing professional move (decision, tradeoff, evidence, uncertainty, consequence, owner, or change trigger).
2. Introduce an adverse consequence or conflicting reviewer lens.
3. Ask what evidence would change the student's position.
4. Record the unresolved learning signal so the instructor can see recurring gaps.

A student can make a non-optimal choice and still demonstrate strong engineering judgment if the choice is explicit, bounded, evidence-backed, proportional to constraints, and revisable. A seemingly optimal choice with no reasoning/evidence is weaker.

## Instructor: Command Center

The instructor view focuses on:

- Team/phase status.
- Review participation and unresolved learning signals.
- Evidence coverage vs. evidence confidence.
- Recurring judgment gaps by phase/dimension.
- Teams requiring instructor verification or intervention.
- Individual role/accountability context without commit-count grading.

The eventual instructor surface should support evidence-linked annotations with provenance labels FACT / REVIEW / POLICY / INSTRUCTOR.
